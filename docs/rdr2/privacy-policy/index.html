MapSlinger

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

MapSlinger is an interactive map of Red Dead Redemption 2 for Android and iOS, made by onthecrow, an independent developer. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-in. The app never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • The markers you find and your settings stay on your device.
  • The app sends usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, to learn which features are used and to fix bugs. They come with a random analytics ID, not with your name.
  • There are no ads. Your data isn't sold or used for advertising.

1. Data that stays on your device

The app keeps on your device:

  • the markers you have marked as found;
  • your settings: theme, colors, language and marker options;
  • the map you had open and its position, the marker categories you show or hide, and the groups you have collapsed in the marker list.

None of it is sent to me or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If backup is on, your device may include it in its backup to your Google or Apple account.

2. Data collected automatically

The app uses the following services, which receive data over encrypted connections.

The usage statistics and crash reports come with an analytics ID: a random code the app creates the first time it's opened. It isn't made from anything on your device or from your accounts. It lets the reports of one installation be put together, and you can find it in Settings → Privacy → Analytics ID to tell it to me when you report a problem or ask about your data. It's deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data; if backup is on, it may come back with the app's data from the backup.

Usage statistics

Google Analytics for Firebase

What
  • the analytics ID, an identifier of the app installation made by Firebase and, on Android, the advertising ID of the device unless you have deleted it;
  • the device model, screen size, operating system and its version, app version and language;
  • your approximate location (country, region and city), which Google derives from your IP address; Google Analytics doesn't store the IP address itself;
  • when and how long you use the app;
  • what you do in the app: the screens and maps you open, the markers you open and mark as found, the categories you show or hide, the settings you change, the links you open, the words you search for, how long the map takes to load and whether its images fail to load.
Why
To learn which features people use and what they look for and can't find, and to improve the app.
Kept
Up to 14 months, then deleted. Aggregated reports, which don't identify installations, may be kept longer.

Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics

What
When the app crashes or runs into an error, such as a map that doesn't load:
  • what the app's code was doing at that moment;
  • what the app did before it: the screens and maps you opened, your actions recorded by the usage statistics, how the maps and the app configuration loaded, and the network errors they ran into;
  • your settings in the app;
  • the device model, operating system and its version, app version, free memory and storage, and the time;
  • an identifier of the app installation and the analytics ID.
Why
To find and fix bugs.
Kept
90 days.

App configuration

Firebase Remote Config

What
An identifier of the app installation, the app version, and the language and region settings of the device.
Why
To download the current list of maps, so maps can be updated without an app update.
Kept
Until it is deleted on request; Google then removes it from its systems within 180 days.

Map display

Google Maps SDK

What
The app draws the game maps with Google Maps SDK, which may send Google information about the device and how the SDK is used. Your use of Google Maps features is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The app doesn't ask for permission to use the location of your device.

Map images

Content servers

What
The map tiles are downloaded from the servers of Rockstar Games (s.rsg.sc), and the images of caves and interiors from the servers of MapGenie (cdn.mapgenie.io). The marker icons are part of the app. As with any website, these servers receive your IP address and standard request details, such as the requested file and the time.

3. What the app doesn't collect

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • the precise location of your device;
  • contacts, photos, files, or access to the camera and microphone;
  • payment information.

The app doesn't track you across apps and websites of other companies. On iOS it doesn't ask for permission to track, so the advertising identifier isn't available to it.

4. Who receives the data

The data described above goes to Google, which provides Firebase and Google Maps. I don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or give it to anyone else, unless the law requires me to.

Google processes data in the United States and other countries. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. You can read more in the Google Privacy Policy, in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Legal basis

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom: usage statistics and crash reports are processed on the basis of legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR), which are keeping the app working and making it better. The data doesn't include your name or contact details, and you can object at any time.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the app stops all collection and deletes the data on your device.
  • To have the usage statistics and crash reports of your installation deleted, send me its analytics ID: tap it in Settings → Privacy to copy it.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset the advertising ID of your device in Settings → Privacy → Ads, or in Settings → Google → Ads on some devices.
  • Depending on where you live, for example in the EEA, the United Kingdom or California, you have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, get a copy of it, restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority.

The app doesn't know who you are, the analytics ID is how I can find your data. Contact me and I'll do what I can.

7. Children

The app is for players of Red Dead Redemption 2 and isn't directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect data of children. If you think the app collected data of a child, contact me and I'll have it deleted.

8. Links to other websites

Marker descriptions and the settings screen have links to other websites, such as YouTube, Telegram, GitHub and Instagram. They open in your browser or their own apps, and their own privacy policies apply.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
